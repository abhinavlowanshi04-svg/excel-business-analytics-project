# 📊 Revenue & Profit Analysis | Excel Dashboard Project

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing sales data to understand business performance through revenue, cost, and profit analysis.

Using Microsoft Excel, I developed an interactive dashboard with Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPI cards, and Slicers to identify sales trends, profitable product categories, regional performance, and sales channel contributions.

The analysis helps identify key business opportunities, understand profitability, and support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyse overall revenue, cost, and profit performance.
- Identify the highest and lowest revenue-generating regions.
- Evaluate profitability across different product categories.
- Compare Offline and Online sales channel performance.
- Analyse yearly revenue and profit trends.
- Identify business challenges and recommend improvement strategies.

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The project focuses on two major analytical areas.

### 1. Revenue Analysis Dashboard

This dashboard analyses revenue performance across different business dimensions.

**Key Analysis:**
- Revenue by Region
- Revenue by Country
- Revenue by Sales Channel
- Yearly Revenue Trends
- Product Category Filtering

**Key Findings:**
- Sub-Saharan Africa generated approximately $357M in revenue.
- Europe generated approximately $353M in revenue.
- Together, these two regions contributed around 54% of total revenue.
- Offline sales generated approximately $699M (53%).
- Online sales generated approximately $628M (47%).
- Revenue peaked in 2014 at approximately $195M.
- North America contributed approximately $25M in revenue.

### 2. Profit Analysis Dashboard

This dashboard evaluates profitability across regions, sales channels, product categories, and time periods.

**Key Analysis:**
- Profit by Region
- Profit by Product Category
- Profit by Sales Channel
- Profit by Country
- Yearly Profit Trends
- Profit Margin Analysis

**Key Findings:**
- Total profit reached approximately $391M.
- Offline sales generated approximately $208M in profit.
- Online sales generated approximately $183M in profit.
- Regional profit margins were relatively consistent, ranging from approximately 28.5% to 31%.
- Profit peaked in 2014 at approximately $57M based on shipping dates.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

### 1. Regional Performance
Europe and Sub-Saharan Africa collectively contribute approximately 54% of total revenue, indicating significant regional concentration.

### 2. Sales Channel Performance
Offline sales contribute 53% of revenue, slightly outperforming Online sales at 47%.

### 3. Product Profitability
Cosmetics is the most profitable product category in terms of profit margin, achieving approximately 39.8%.

Office Supplies generates the highest revenue at approximately $289M but has a relatively low profit margin of 19.4%.

### 4. Revenue Trends
Revenue reached its highest point in 2014 at approximately $195M. Revenue subsequently declined through 2016.

### 5. Product Margin Differences
Profit margins vary significantly across product categories:

- Clothes: 67.2%
- Cereal: 43.1%
- Vegetables: 41.0%
- Cosmetics: 39.8%
- Office Supplies: 19.4%
- Meat: 13.6%

This indicates that product mix plays an important role in overall profitability.

## ⚠️ Business Challenges & Recommendations

| Business Challenge | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| High revenue dependency on two regions | Explore growth opportunities in Asia, Central America, and Oceania. |
| Low profit margin in Meat | Review pricing and cost structures to improve profitability. |
| Revenue decline after 2014 | Investigate declining product and country performance. |
| Limited North American orders | Conduct targeted marketing campaigns to explore growth opportunities. |
| Low profitability in Office Supplies | Review pricing strategies and operational costs. |

**Potential Improvement Opportunity:**

Increasing the Meat category's profit margin from 13.6% to approximately 19.4% could generate an estimated additional $10M in profit, assuming revenue and other factors remain unchanged.

## ⚙️ Project Methodology

The project followed these steps:

1. **Data Understanding:** Examined the dataset, its columns, and business dimensions.
2. **Data Analysis:** Analysed revenue, cost, and profit across different categories.
3. **Pivot Tables:** Created summary tables for region, country, product, sales channel, and year.
4. **Pivot Charts:** Used visualisations to represent business performance and trends.
5. **Dashboard Development:** Designed Revenue and Profit analysis dashboards using KPI cards and charts.
6. **Interactive Analysis:** Added an Item Type slicer to filter dashboard visualisations.
7. **Business Recommendations:** Converted analytical findings into actionable recommendations.
